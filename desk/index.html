    How to read this

    Edge is how much more often a strategy's picks moved the right way than the market's own base rate for that day. It is not a return. For the lab's first provisional rule, its blended score (Champion v1), the average move of its picks after Indian trading costs was slightly negative in the train years (between −0.12 and −0.54 percentage points), so a hit-rate edge is a research finding, not a claim about money made.

    Why there is a correction. The lab tried many variants. Run enough of them and some look good by luck, so every result here is checked again after counting how many were tried. Intervals are built so that days that move together are not counted as independent.

    How this was tested

    The joint test is Hansen's SPA (read at 5%), the one-by-one check is the Romano-Wolf stepwise test (StepM, at 10%), and the model confidence set (MCS, at 10%) keeps the variants not clearly worse than the best. Intervals come from a stationary block bootstrap over dates.

    Limits. Train years only, so none of the lab's strategy results here is validated on unseen data yet. The weekly result is read from the lab's evidence file each time. That file is rebuilt by the lab's own workflow and can lag behind its newest experiments, which are not counted here until the next successful build. This page reads the lab's file each time.
