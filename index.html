Does a Gaussian (parametric) 1-day Value-at-Risk model actually hold up against the real exception rate, once it’s backtested rather than just computed?

The Kupiec test rejects the 1-day parametric VaR at 99% confidence (10/500 exceptions vs. 5.0 expected, LR = 3.914, p = 0.048).

Research

How Test-Set Peeking Inflates Benchmark Comparisons

Submitted to a conference, 2026

Comparing a new method against the “best single model” picked after looking at the test score gives that pick an unfair, partly-luck advantage. CRAFT, a machine-learning pipeline built for a larger project, is used to measure how large that advantage is, against a stable cross-validation baseline that never looks at the test score. Across 163 public benchmark collections, swapping which model is compared against changes which one looks ahead in 31% of cases.
